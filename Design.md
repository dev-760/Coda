## Brand & Style - Luma v2.0

The design system embodies the calculated restraint and tactile permanence of modern industrial audio engineering combined with the measured whitespace of contemporary minimalist design. It is built for discerning listeners who prioritize sonic immersion and intelligent recommendations.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on absolute functional clarity:
- **Quiet Authority**: Every interaction is purposeful, deliberate, and silent until engaged.
- **Intelligent Discovery**: AI-powered recommendations seamlessly integrated into the listening experience.
- **Modern Precision**: Controls echo contemporary design with smooth animations and intuitive gestures.
- **Professional Quality**: Production-ready UI with attention to detail and user experience.

Strict visual taboos:
- No neon accents, glowing laser rings, or RGB drop shadows.
- No saturated purple, cyan, or acid-green states.
- No pure pitch blacks (`#000000`) or decorative circular capsules encasing every standard action.

## Colors - Luma Palette

The palette relies on the Luma custom color scheme with a dark structural base balanced against desaturated steel and accent colors.

### Luma Custom Colors
- **Luma Dark**: `#090B10` — The foundational backdrop. Never absolute zero black.
- **Luma Surface**: `#0D1017` — Primary panel backdrops and structural decks.
- **Luma Elevated**: `#11151D` — Elevated grouping cards, nested channels, and active drawer surfaces.
- **Luma White**: `#F3F5F8` — Sharp, clear, low-glare white for titles and critical metrics.
- **Luma Gray**: `#AEB6C3` — Balanced slate gray for tracklists, metadata, and active modes.
- **Luma Muted**: `#737D8C` — Subdued warm gray for runtimes, timestamps, and unit badges.
- **Luma Accent**: `#8D8170` — Warm matte accent for primary playback indicators and active states.
- **Luma Steel**: `#6F7E88` — Desaturated steel-sage for secondary accents and queue elements.

### Structural Foundations
- **Base Canvas**: `#090B10` — The foundational backdrop.
- **Surface Elevation 1**: `#0D1017` — Primary panel backdrops and structural decks.
- **Surface Elevation 2**: `#11151D` — Elevated grouping cards, nested channels, and active drawer surfaces.
- **Surface Elevation 3**: `#1A1F29` — Dropdown menus, floating parameter bars, and overlay popovers.
- **Surface Elevation 4**: `#252B37` — Scrubber tracks, active component wells, and tactile toggles.

### Line Work & Separation
- **Subtle Partition**: `#4A5463` — Fine hairline splitters separating modular audio channels.
- **Border Default**: `#737D8C` — Structural outlines defining container boundaries.
- **Border Active**: `#6F7E88` — Focused, selected, or engaged boundary edges.

### Typography Hierarchy
- **Text Primary**: `#F3F5F8` — Sharp, clear, low-glare white for titles, artist indices, and critical metrics.
- **Text Secondary**: `#AEB6C3` — Balanced slate gray for tracklists, metadata, and active modes.
- **Text Muted**: `#737D8C` — Subdued warm gray for runtimes, timestamps, and mechanical unit badges.

### Accent Principles
- **Primary Accent (`#6F7E88`)**: Luma Steel. Used for primary actions, queue elements, and navigation.
- **Secondary Accent (`#8D8170`)**: Luma Accent. Used with restraint for timeline playheads, primary playback indicators, and active selector pins.

## Typography

The type system is governed by **SpaceGrotesk** for display and body text, and **CourierPrime** for monospace elements, leveraging their modern discipline and engineered geometry to convey high-end apparatus calibration.

### Rules of Usage
- **Numerals & Metrics**: All runtimes, durations, decibel readouts, and sampling rates must utilize CourierPrime monospace to ensure absolute layout stability during playback scrub or volume shifts.
- **Labels & Badges**: Small sub-headings, section badges, and format descriptors are rendered in `label-sm` or `label-md` with uppercase transformations and loose tracking.
- **Editorial Titles**: Track, album, and artist headers employ SpaceGrotesk with tight letter-spacing to yield a modern, professional appearance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 8pt rhythmic matrix, maintaining geometric balance across compact views and wide mixing decks.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: The main layout splits into an asymmetric 3-column chassis: Collapsible library navigation (2.5 cols), Main stage / Artwork / Liner notes (6 cols), and Inspection / Queue / Hardware telemetry (3.5 cols). Margin is anchored at `margin-desktop` (3rem) with `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem).
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Queue and master deck collapse into an expansive bottom drawer, keeping primary artwork and track controls uninterrupted.
- **Mobile (Single Column)**: Strict dynamic padding respecting device safe areas. The primary playback deck remains fixed at the foot with edge-to-edge scrubbing.

Spacing within component boundaries relies entirely on strict inner padding: `space-xs` (4px) for control cluster gaps, `space-sm` (8px) between icon and metadata, and `space-lg` (24px) for audio channel separation.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects heavy diffuse dropshadows and blur-heavy consumer glassmorphism. Depth is created through **physical structural stepping** and **chiseled mechanical borders**.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base - `#090B10`)**: The monolithic deck. Sits furthest back.
- **Level 1 (Module Plates - `#0D1017`)**: Structural partitions framed by `1px solid #1C222D`. Zero shadow.
- **Level 2 (Inlaid Elements - `#11151D`)**: Recessed trays, interactive cards, and album artwork receptacles. Finished with `1px solid #252C38`.
- **Level 3 (Tactile Actuators & Menus - `#161B24`)**: Precision popovers and contextual cue sheets. Elevated using a single surgical ambient shadow: `0px 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55)` bounded by a subtle top-lit hairline border (`1px solid #303846`).
- **Engraved Channels**: Timeline tracks and volume sliders use an inset illusion created by a dark inner stroke (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`) within `#1B212C`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (`1`)** shape language:
- Base container radius is strictly `0.25rem` (4px).
- Larger media tiles, vinyl jacket holders, and modal sheets use `0.5rem` (8px, `rounded-lg`).
- Sliders and status pips use full circular pill caps only where physical mechanics dictate (e.g., dial handles, thumb notches).

Sharp, disciplined outer corners reinforce the feeling of milled aluminum and CNC-machined instruments. Under no circumstances should buttons or cards be rounded into bubbly circular lozenges.

## Components

### Buttons & Transport Controls
- **Master Play / Pause**: A pristine physical token. Solid off-white `#F3F5F8` background with an obsidian `#090B10` playback glyph. Dimensions: 44x44px, `rounded-lg` (8px radius). Micro-interaction: Depresses downward by 1px on active state (`transform: translateY(1px)`) with no glowing drop shadows.
- **Secondary Controls (Skip, Shuffle, Loop, Mute)**: Bare, high-precision vector iconography with a uniform `1.6px` stroke width in `#AEB6C3`. Hover transitions to `#F3F5F8` over 120ms linear. No background container pill.
- **Auxiliary System Buttons**: Background `#11151D`, border `1px solid #252C38`, text `#F3F5F8`, 4px radius. On hover: border steps to `#303846`, background to `#161B24`.

### Playback Scrubber & Volume Faders
- **Track Rail**: A 2px high channel set to `#1B212C`.
- **Elapsed Track Fill**: Solid warm muted accent `#8D8170`.
- **Scrubber Thumb**: A minimal 10x10px milled square or 4x14px vertical hairline wedge, colored `#F3F5F8`. Shows only during hover or direct engagement.
- **Time Indicators**: Rendered in `mono-num` typography in `#737D8C`, flanking the rail with identical fixed widths.

### Tracklist & Table Rows
- **Layout**: Hairline border `#1C222D` between rows. Height: 48px.
- **Columns**: Track index (`#737D8C`), Title (`#F3F5F8`), Artist (`#AEB6C3`), File Specification (`#737D8C`, mono), Duration (`#737D8C`, mono).
- **Active State**: Row background shifts to `#0D1017`, left edge displays a 2px vertical indicator bar in `#8D8170`, title text shifts to `#8D8170`.

### Input Fields & Parameter Steppers
- **Frame**: `#0D1017` background with a crisp `1px solid #252C38` border.
- **Focus**: Border turns to `#8D8170` with no soft outer focus halo. Caret color `#8D8170`.
- **Text**: `#F3F5F8`, placeholder `#737D8C`.

### Metadata & Format Chips
- Monochromatic, compact indicators (`DSD 256`, `FLAC`, `96kHz`).
- Background `#11151D`, border `1px solid #1C222D`, corner radius `2px`. Text is `label-sm` in `#AEB6C3`. Never colored with saturated status tags.

### Physical VU Meter / Decibel Telemetry (Specialized Audio Component)
- Precision segmented bar graph composed of 1px ticks spaced 1.5px apart.
- Nominal levels (-inf to -3dB) illuminate in `#6F7E88` (Luma Steel).
- Peak levels (-2dB to 0dB) step into warm pigment amber `#8D8170` (Luma Accent).
- Overdrive (+1dB and up) illuminates in muted earthen brick `#9A806A`. No garish red warnings.
